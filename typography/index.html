Typography

Onest carries display and body alike. Redaction 35 italic is the accent - on single words, never a whole block. Mono is for labels and numbers.

Used onEvery page

Families

--sansOnest - display & body
Variable 400..600 from Google Fonts. A subrange request: 7 @font-face rules instead of 21.
--serifRedaction 35 - accents
Italic 400 only, from jsDelivr (OFL). The number is how destroyed the face is; cleaner is downward, and 35 is settled.
--monoui-monospace - labels, numbers, code
The system stack. Uppercase, tracked, 12px.

Scale

Seven steps and nothing between them. 18px is the floor for anything meant to be read; the 12px mono label sits outside the scale because it is chrome, not copy.

Weights

TokenValueRoleStatus
--w-body400Body and Redaction accents.Shipped
--w-head450Every heading and heading-ish <b>. A real instance of Onest's variable axis.Shipped
--w-strong600Inline <strong> and the wordmark only.Shipped

450 is a genuine instance of Onest's variable axis, not a synthesised in-between: a width probe gives 746.25 / 749.77 / 753.16 / 759.69px at 400 / 450 / 500 / 600, strictly monotonic.

Rules

Do
  • Accent one word per heading with Redaction - the word the sentence turns on.
  • Use tabular numbers for anything that counts.
  • Prove a webfont loads with a width probe, never by eye.
Don't
  • Set a whole paragraph or pull quote in Redaction - the pull quote is Onest italic.
  • Ask Redaction for 500 or 600: only italic 400 is declared, anything else synthesises.
  • Use clamp() for type.
