Interface sounds for the Pixel and Aurora voices: arcade/ and zen/ from the
uisfx 0.4.0 npm package (Romain Simon). The audio is dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Only the cues the voices use are kept here; js/sound-ui.js plays them.

kolam/ - the Kolam voice. Every cue is a single stroke or short phrase cut from
mridangam recordings by ajaysm on Freesound ("Mridangam-Khanda", 176276, and
"Mridangam Jati", 194579), both CC0 1.0. The success cue is cut from "Indian
Temple Bell" by ganiket (466652), CC0 1.0. Levelled and faded at the ends.

../audio/kl-bed.m4a is 93 seconds of "Veena_Recording" by iskweldog (216060),
CC0 1.0, looped with a crossfade. ../audio/kl-koel.m4a is "Koel-01" by
ramblinglibrarian (460466), CC0 1.0.
