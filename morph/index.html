Morph button

One control, every state. The button becomes its own progress readout, then its result, then grows into the panel it opened - no new window, no separate bar.

Animation speed
S

The whole journey

Pressed, the label gives way to a progress readout inside the same shape; the shape settles on the result; then it grows into the panel, and folds back on close. Each world plays the states its own way.

States

StateWhat the shape doesWhat is announced
idleThe button at rest, sized to its label.Its label.
busyNarrows or widens to the readout: ring, verb, percentage. The fill drops to a quiet surface, so it reads as status, not as a second button.The verb, once, politely. aria-busy on the button.
doneThe result in the success colour. The world may celebrate.The result.
openThe button's own box grows into the panel (a shared-element view transition). Focus moves into the panel; Escape or Close folds it back and returns focus.The panel's heading, by focus.

Rules

  • One surface per job: never open a second progress bar for something a button started.
  • Width animates; height never jumps. The faces stack in one grid cell.
  • The readout never lies: the percentage is the job's, and the pause near three quarters is real work, not theatre.
  • Reduced motion: states swap instantly and the panel appears without the grow.
