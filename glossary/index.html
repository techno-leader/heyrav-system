Behaviour

  • A real <button> with aria-expanded and aria-controls; the box is hidden until opened.
  • One open at a time; outside click or Escape closes and returns focus.
  • The Phosphor Info glyph is one shape, so nothing sits off-centre the way a bordered "i" did.

Use it for one or two terms per page. If every paragraph needs one, the copy needs rewriting instead.
