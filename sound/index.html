Foundations

Sound

Sound confirms what the hand just did. It stays off until a visitor turns it on, sits under the volume of a room, and never plays on hover or scroll.

This world's voice

Core

Short tones and soft clicks, synthesised in the browser from a few numbers each. No audio files: every sound in this world weighs less than a kilobyte.

Under it, the depth gauge in the corner has the sound of the sea, with its own switch: water all the way down and one voice at each depth.

Retro, with Arcade

Square-wave blips, synthesised in the browser like an 8-bit console. Opening, closing, the theme and the video player come from Arcade: short chiptune files, because Retro has no sound for them.

Core and Zen

The small touches keep Core's soft clicks. Everything that moves or finishes speaks Zen: pure tones and dry wood, as small recorded files. The corner has the sound of the night, wind over snow, with its own switch.

Mridangam

Recorded, not synthesised. Every touch, move and result is a stroke of the mridangam, the two-headed drum of Carnatic music, and a success rings a temple bell. While sound is on, a veena plays low under the page, with a koel now and then.

Where sound goes

Three weights, from the lightest. Every control here plays its sound in this world's voice, even with the speaker off.

Touch

A control changed state under the finger. Under 80ms, at about half volume.

PressA button commits something: save, export, send. Buttons that only go somewhere stay silent.
SwitchA setting flips: rising as it turns on, falling as it turns off.
CheckboxOne item joins or leaves a set.
SelectOne option out of a few: segment, chip, tab or radio.
Slider stepOn whole steps only, never on every pixel of a drag.

Move

Something opened, closed or changed place. Under 250ms.

ExpandAn accordion opens, or the morph button grows into its panel.
Is there a dark theme?
Yes, with data-theme="dark".
OpenA menu, popover or lightbox arrives over the page, then leaves.
SearchThe ⌘K palette opens.
ThemeLight to dark falls, dark to light rises. The page stays as it is here.
WorldThe whole site re-tunes: the one move that earns a longer sweep.
VideoPlay and pause on the film player.

Result

Work finished or failed. The only weight allowed a tail, and still under 600ms.

CopyCode, a token or a link lands on the clipboard, and the icon turns to a tick.
Saved or failedA toast and its sound arrive together.
SendThe edit widget raises a pull request.
Remove and undoA block is taken out of the page, then put back.

Silent on purpose

  • Hover and focus. They happen dozens of times a minute without intent.
  • Scroll, reveals and links. Motion and the new page already answer.
  • Typing. The keyboard makes its own sound.
  • Anything nobody did. Autoplay, timers and background saves. Sound follows a hand.

Rules

  • Off until asked. The speaker at the foot of the sidebar turns sound on for the whole site, remembered per device. Until then no page loads a sound.
  • One voice per world. A second source only fills a cue the first has no sound for.
  • One result per action, never alone. A copy that also raises a toast is heard once, and every sound has a visible change with it.
  • Clean under load. The same cue never twice in 40ms, nothing while the tab is hidden, and a limiter so two cues together never clip.

Use

Components carry no sound code. One script listens to what they already do and answers in the current world's voice.

<script src="js/sound-ui.js" defer></script>
<button data-snd-toggle aria-pressed="false">…</button>  <!-- the visitor's switch -->

HRSoundUI.cue('success');   // a cue the page cannot see, in the world's voice
HRSound.volume(0.5);        // master, 0 to 1
