Keycap

A key you can press. It sinks while held and springs back past rest, and the pulse behind it shows the world that comes next.

Animation speed
S
Press R for a new world · Esc to go back
Show code
<div class="d-keycap"><button class="keycap" type="button" data-world-random data-glyph="mark" aria-keyshortcuts="r" aria-label="A random world"><span class="keycap-glyph">R</span></button><span class="keycap-note">Press R for a new world · Esc to go back</span></div>

Anatomy

  • Plate. A dotted outline on the page, with a hole cut in it for the key.
  • Cap. The part that travels. Held, it goes most of the way into the hole and gives a little; let go and it springs back past rest.
  • Lip. The front edge of the hole, drawn over the cap, so the cap goes into the plate rather than squashing.
  • Pulse. Rings in the next world's colours ripple out of the hole, so the next look is never a surprise.

Behaviour

  • Held, it stays down. Mouse, touch and the R key itself all press it, and the world changes as it is pressed. The new world spreads out from the key.
  • Every world once. R walks through all the worlds in a shuffled order before any repeats, and the next one loads while the current one is on screen.
  • Esc goes back to the world before the first press, once nothing else is open for it to close.
  • Not while typing. R in a field is a letter. Reduced motion keeps the press and drops the spring and the pulse.

This world's key

An outlined key in warm off-white, drawn in hairlines on a dotted plate, with a short spring.

A bonus block: square and gold, four rivets, a hard outline, and it moves in frames, not tweens.

A stack of card cut out of the page along a dashed line, its layers showing on the sides.

Frosted glass over a hole that glows with the sky's green and violet, slow to sink and slow to rise.

A rounded pebble of sea glass with a highlight on top. It bobs on the way back up.

A washed stone tile with its dots laid, the letter in kaavi, its sides stained with red earth.
